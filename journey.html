01b — Journey

Zigong → Chengdu → New York → San Jose

A quick map of how a kid from a small city in Sichuan ended up designing power management chips on two continents.

  • 29.35°N, 104.78°E

    Zigong

    Sichuan, China · Where it started

    Born and raised in Zigong — a small industrial city in Sichuan known for salt mining, dinosaur fossils, and lantern festivals. First taste of taking things apart to see how they work.

  • 30.66°N, 104.07°E

    Chengdu

    Sichuan, China · 2016 – 2020

    Moved to the provincial capital for a B.S. in Electrical Engineering at UESTC — roughly 180 km from home, but a first real step away. This is where circuits stopped being a subject and became the thing I wanted to do.

  • 40.71°N, 74.01°W

    New York

    United States · 2021 – present

    ~11,700 km from Chengdu for an M.S. & Ph.D. at Columbia University. New country, new language of instruments, same question: how do you keep power clean and efficient when everything around it is switching?

  • 37.34°N, 121.89°W

    San Jose

    California, United States · Summer 2026

    ~4,100 km west to Samsung's DRAM Design Lab for a circuit design internship — from lecture halls in Manhattan to the middle of Silicon Valley's memory industry.
